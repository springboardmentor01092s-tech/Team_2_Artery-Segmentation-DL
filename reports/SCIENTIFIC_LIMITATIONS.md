# Scientific limitations — 16 November 2026

- No verified exposure-cleared independent patient cohort.
- No qualified blinded calibrated MLD/RVD or QCA reference set.
- No acquisition-specific calibration for the preserved XCA case; pixels are correctly retained.
- No matched independent expert lesion severity set under a locked protocol.
- No eligible native-resolution CTA lumen masks with independent stenosis measurements.
- ImageCAS/ImageCAS-X split intersections prevent treating those variants as independent cohorts.
- Checkpoint-specific XAI faithfulness/randomization evidence is incomplete.
- Synthetic known-truth tests establish software geometry behavior only, not clinical accuracy.

The correct characterization is an engineering-verified research prototype, not a clinically validated system.
