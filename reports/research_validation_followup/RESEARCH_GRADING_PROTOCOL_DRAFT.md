# Research grading protocol draft

## Recommended evidence-based starting point

Keep the published CAD-RADS 2.0 anatomical terms as a separate six-category reference: 0% no visible stenosis, 1-24% minimal, 25-49% mild, 50-69% moderate, 70-99% severe and 100% occluded. CAD-RADS is a CCTA, per-patient reporting system; CoroVision must not call its XCA lesion output CAD-RADS.

The project PDF requests five labels. Any merging or renaming is an experimental adaptation requiring guide approval. The existing `EXPERIMENTAL_FIVE_CATEGORY_PROTOCOL_PROPOSAL.json` remains `proposed`, has no approver/date and is rejected by the active grading engine.

## Approval decision required

The guide should choose one of:

1. approve a separate six-category research categorization with explicit XCA adaptation limitations; or
2. approve a five-category experimental mapping, documenting which published categories are combined and how total occlusion is handled.

Assignment uses the unrounded percentage. Bands are lower-inclusive and upper-exclusive, except that 100 is included in the final band. An approved file must retain modality/anatomy applicability, measurement method, exclusions, source references, approver identity and approval date.
